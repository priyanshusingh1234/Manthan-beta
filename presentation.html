🔍 The Context

Students preparing for high-stakes exams face two conflicting forces: the immense psychological pressure of isolated rote learning, and the constant digital distraction of unmoderated social media algorithms designed to steal attention.

04  //  Key Insights / Core Problem

Synthesizing The Gap

👤

Insight 1: User Reality

Students do not hate learning; they hate isolated learning. They crave the social mechanics, virtual economies, and competition of gaming, but need it channeled into an academic proof-of-work system to feel productive.

💳

Insight 2: Payer Reality

The biggest flaw in current edtech is the payer-user disconnect. Parents will readily fund digital communities, but only if they are heavily moderated and provide transparent, data-driven academic returns to justify the cost.

Core Problem Statement

"Students lack a safe, distraction-free digital ecosystem that leverages social gamification to drive consistent practice, while institutions and parents lack the diagnostic tools to monitor that progress securely."

05  //  Detailed Solution Description

Dheeyudha: The Unified Academic Ecosystem

1 The Core Engine (B2C)

A gamified academic network where access to the community feed is strictly gated behind a daily "Tiny Gauntlet" (a 10-question rapid test). Students earn virtual points to unlock premium notes in a peer-help economy.

2 Tech Architecture

A production-ready ecosystem architected on a unified Next.js & React Native frontend with a Supabase backend. Features Live AI Grading via Gemini, real-time syncing, and strict Row Level Security (RLS).

3 Institutional Distribution

Schools receive a Principal Dashboard and provide students with "School Codes." In return, the school gets automated diagnostic data tracking batch performance, transforming institutions into zero-cost user acquisition engines.

4 Monetization Engine

Dual-Value Micro-Subscription:

₹99/month unlocks Tier 2 advanced questions for students and activates the deep-analytics Parent Portal.

Institutional AdTech:

Coaching institutes pay premium placement fees for admission banners targeting high-performing students.
